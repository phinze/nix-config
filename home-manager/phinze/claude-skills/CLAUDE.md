# Writing skills

A skill is standing instruction that agents read cold, in situations
nobody has imagined yet. Write the rule, not the story that taught it.

- State the behavior and why it matters. Leave out the incident that
  prompted it: dates, PR and ticket numbers, rig names, who said what.
- If the only justification for a rule is one scenario, it isn't a rule
  yet. Leave it out and let Paul steer case by case until it recurs.
- Examples earn their place when they show a reusable shape (a command,
  a message template, a before/after), not when they record what
  happened once.
